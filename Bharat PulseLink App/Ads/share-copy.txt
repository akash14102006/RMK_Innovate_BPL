2. LINKEDIN (Official Launch Announcement)
================================================================================
Today, we are proud to introduce Bharat PulseLink: India’s Shared Memory for Every Patient, Every Hospital.

In a healthcare ecosystem as vast and diverse as India, patient care often faces three major barriers:
1. Fragmented health records scattered across paper files and disconnected clinics.
2. Repetitive, manual front-desk hospital registration queues.
3. Digital exclusion of 400M+ citizens who rely on feature phones.

Bharat PulseLink bridges this divide:
🔹 Dual-Channel Access: Modern smartphone app + regional language Toll-Free IVR phone booking.
🔹 Zero Patient Fees: 100% free for all patients. Built for and subscribed to by hospitals.
🔹 Privacy by Architecture: Dynamic QR codes containing ZERO raw medical or Aadhaar data—only transient cryptographic handshake tokens.
🔹 Continuous Care: Lab reports, digital prescriptions, and discharge summaries follow the patient seamlessly.

Because healthcare should remember the patient—wherever they go.
